# Premium Due Reminder: how to use it

## One-time setup (10 minutes)
1. **Add columns to your customer Excel:** `Premium Due Date` (required), plus `Policy No`, `Premium Amount` and `Frequency` (Monthly / Quarterly / Half-yearly / Yearly) if you have them. Keep your existing columns: Customer Name, Company, Birthday, Phone, Email, Account Manager. Header names don't need to match exactly.
2. **Open the app on your phone** and tap **Import customer list**. Pick the .xlsx or .csv file. Check the column matches, then tap **Yes, use this list**.
3. **Settings tab:** set your name/team (it signs the message), currency (e.g. RM), and country code (numbers starting with 0 need it for WhatsApp).
4. Tap **Add 5th & 25th to calendar** once, so your phone reminds you on every check-in day.

## Every 5th and 25th
1. Open the app. The top card shows who is due before the next check-in (plus 3 days).
2. **Remind today** (orange) = due within 3 days. Tap **Remind**, check the message (English + Chinese), and tap **Open WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with the text filled in. **You** press Send.
3. Back in the app, tap **Yes, mark as reminded**.
4. **Coming up** (blue) customers show the date to remind them (3 days before due). Open the app again that day, or send early.
5. Tap **Download PDF** for the full list: a summary page, then one page per customer with both messages and their ePartner screenshot.

## ePartner screenshots
In ePartner, open the customer's details page and take a screenshot. In the app, tap the customer → **Details** → **Add screenshot**. It goes into their PDF page. WhatsApp links can only carry text, so use **Save page as image** to attach it in the chat.

## When something is wrong
The **To check** tab lists anyone the app won't guess about: missing or impossible due dates (e.g. 31/02), missing or too-short phone numbers, duplicate policy numbers, unknown frequency. Tap **Fix**, correct it, and confirm. Every save and delete asks you first.

## Good to know
- Customer data stays in your phone's browser only. Using a different phone or browser means importing again.
- PDF or photo customer lists aren't read automatically, so nothing gets misread. Open them in Excel or copy them into the blank template (Settings → Download blank template).
- Use the **As of** date at the top to preview any future check-in.
